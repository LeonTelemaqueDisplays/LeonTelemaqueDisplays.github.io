About

Player behaviour is not a mystery. It's a system.

The data tells you where retention broke. The behaviour tells you why.

I study why players behave the way they do inside the systems studios design for them — LiveOps event health, retention curves, and the monetisation layer that decides whether spending feels worth it. I pull those apart to understand what's actually driving the number.

Then I build the data layer that proves it. SQL, Python, Streamlit — from scratch, schema to dashboard. Most people who think about player behaviour don't build the analytics. Most people who build the analytics don't think about player behaviour. I do both.

On the analysis side I track event cadence, cohort retention, and frustration signals across live-service products — building the case for exactly where in the player lifecycle engagement collapses, and which behavioural signals predict it before it happens.

On the systems side I think in economic layers — spend-tier segmentation, offer framing, scarcity design — modelling how different player archetypes respond to different design levers, and where the line to predatory design actually sits.

The two sides are not separate disciplines. The telemetry tells you where the system broke. The system design tells you why the telemetry looks the way it does.
