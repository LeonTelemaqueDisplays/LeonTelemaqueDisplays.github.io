03 · Analyst Note
Core Finding
"Commuter behaviour is driven more by perceived friction and decision complexity than by objective transit efficiency. The Crosstown's value is not primarily speed — it is the elimination of ambiguity."
→ NOTE
Why this matters beyond transit: The same friction model applies to any onboarding or engagement system. In gaming, players churn not when content is hard — but when the path forward is ambiguous. In fintech, users avoid action not when stakes are high — but when decision complexity overwhelms context. The Crosstown analysis is, functionally, a retention problem framed in urban infrastructure.
