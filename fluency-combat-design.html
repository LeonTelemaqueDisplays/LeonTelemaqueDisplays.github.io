Game Design Systems · Combat Design Document

Fluency

MMO Combat, Identity & the Language of Mastery
Early Development

MMOs are power fantasies built around individual identity. Yet most MMO combat systems express neither — they reward rotation memorisation and stat thresholds over reactive decision making and personal expression. Fluency is a prototype exploring what MMO combat looks like when progression measures mastery and identity, not just power.

01
Layer 01

The Argument

Modern MMO combat has a formula. It works. But studios that copy it without evolution produce combat that feels passive, mechanical, and alienating to a console audience raised on action games. Fluency argues that the WoW foundation — telegraphed attacks, resource management, rotational structure — is not the problem. The absence of reactivity and individual expression built on top of that foundation is.

Fluency is built around a single design question — what does MMO combat look like when mastery changes the player's combat identity rather than just their damage output? Two players at the same progression level with different skill investments should fight the same enemy in visibly different ways. Not because one is stronger. Because they understood the system differently and built toward a different expression of it.

Progression should change how you fight, who you are, and how hard you hit. Not just the last one.
02
Layer 02

The Three Progression States

01
Early State
Learning the Language
The player enters combat with a light combo, heavy combo, two abilities, parry, and dodge. Combat is about reading and reacting correctly. Simple enough to learn, deep enough to feel rewarding when executed well. The foundation that never goes away regardless of how far progression advances.
02
Mid State
Learning the Grammar
Elemental attributes, status effects, and two additional abilities are added. Combat now requires two simultaneous layers — reactive reads through parry and dodge, and strategic decisions about elemental and status application. The skill ceiling rises through decision making not just reaction. The player is no longer just responding — they are choosing.
03
Late State
Developing a Voice
Effective mastery of all mechanics and optimal use of acquired skills creates individuality. Two players at the same progression level fight the same enemy in visibly different ways. Not because one is stronger — because they understood the system differently and built toward different expressions of mastery. This is the demo's thesis made visible.
03
Layer 03

The Class System

Three classes with distinct combat philosophies, all built on the same underlying system. Class identity is a starting point, not a ceiling.

Vanguard
The Greatsword
Bulkier and slower
Combat is about patience, positioning, and creating openings through deliberate reads. Each attack carries significant weight — elemental application is intentional rather than incidental. The Vanguard absorbs and counters where other classes would dodge and disengage.
Enforcer
The Broadsword
Primary damage dealer, high mobility
Fast paced and aggressive. High hit rate makes the Enforcer a natural status applicator through volume. Combat is about exploiting windows created by their own mobility and the openings left by enemy animations.
Support
Folly / Mage
Fate manipulation via card play
The Folly is a card-based weapon unique to the Support class, revolving around fortune and misfortune. Fortune applies Tempered as a buff to allies — an all-around stat increase. Misfortune applies Tempered as a debuff to enemies — an all-around stat reduction. The Support controls the statistical tide of the fight by distributing fate to both sides of the encounter.
Mage weapon style — stretch goal, pending animation sourcing

Layer 04

The Skill System

Inspired by Sword Art Online and Where Winds Meet — Fluency does not use a traditional node-based skill tree. Skills are acquired through world interaction and quest completion, universal to all players and builds. Weapon-specific skills unlock progressively through mastery of the equipped weapon.

Combo Extender
Extends the light or heavy combo string. Each class interprets this differently based on their combat foundation.
Self Buff
Temporary personal stat increase. Context and timing of use varies significantly across class identities.
Status Application
A dedicated status delivery skill outside the normal combo structure. Gives every class reliable, on-demand status effect access.

Four per class, tied to the equipped weapon and unlocked progressively through weapon mastery. Not detailed at design document stage — developed during implementation.

05
Layer 05

The Status System

Five status effects with distinct functions. All status effects find application across all classes — individuality comes from which effects a player prioritises and how they apply them.

Paralysis
ELECTRIC STUN
Halts enemy movement. Creates windows for combo execution. Most immediately useful to Enforcers and Supports setting up team opportunities.
Poison
DAMAGE OVER TIME
Constant tick damage with defense reduction. Consistent damage amplification. An Enforcer who constantly reapplies poison through mobility builds a damage-over-time layer on top of burst damage.
Burn
DAMAGE OVER TIME
Constant tick damage. Pairs with high-mobility Enforcer hit-and-run patterns. Applied as a secondary damage source by Support alongside other debuff management.
Frostbite
DEFENSE REDUCTION · SLOW
Defense reduction and movement slow. Compensates for Vanguard's heavier movement by creating easier parry windows. Enforcer uses the defense reduction to maximise burst in short windows.
Tempered
SUPPORT EXCLUSIVE
Moderate all-around stat reduction on enemies through misfortune, all-around stat increase on allies through fortune. Breadth of effect over depth — the ultimate setup debuff that enables both Vanguard pressure and Enforcer burst.

When two elements are applied near-simultaneously, a reaction effect triggers. Example — Burn and Frostbite applied in sequence produce Sheer Cold, a reaction damage effect neither element produces alone.

Elemental reactions reward player experimentation and deepen the system without requiring immediate comprehension. Planned as a final polish pass after core systems are functional.

Layer 07

The Demo Structure

Enemy — one elite enemy with a simple attack set designed to demand different reads and responses from each class. The enemy is the constant. The classes are the variable.

Environment — grey box space with grass floor. The environment is irrelevant to the argument. Viewer attention stays on the system.

Vanguard · Standard
Patience & Counter
Slow greatsword identity, patience and counter-based combat, deliberate elemental application.
Vanguard · Variant
Speed & Elements
Attack speed and elemental application build. Same class, same weapon, completely different combat rhythm. Individuality within a single class made visible.
Enforcer · Build One
Stun & Poison
Paralysis into constant poison reapplication through mobility. Defense-shredding hit-and-run identity.
Enforcer · Build Two
Frostbite & Burn
Slow the enemy then apply burn for sustained damage while compromised. More calculated than the stun/poison build despite same class and weapon.
Support · Build One
Buffer & Debuffer
Fortune and misfortune balanced. Managing ally buffs and enemy debuffs simultaneously through the Folly card system. Strategic depth at its fullest.
Support · Build Two
Full Offensive
All in on damage application through Arcana and Tempered misfortune stacking. The support toolkit used aggressively rather than supportively. Same weapon, completely different intent.
Skill System Visualisation
Each archetype's acquired universal skills and weapon-specific skills displayed alongside the combat demonstration. The viewer sees what the character has learned and how it maps to their combat identity. UX/UI work required.
Win Condition
The viewer walks away understanding what creates individuality in a class system. Not different characters. Not different weapons.
The same underlying system expressed differently — through mastery, skill acquisition, and deliberate build choices.
Progression that changes who you are, not just how hard you hit.
