How I Work

Process? Yeah, I have one.

Every case study on this site follows the same four moves — whether it's a two-day teardown or a six-week build.

Frame the Question
State the assumption or decision under scrutiny, and what's actually at stake if it's wrong.
Build the Model
SQL, Python, Streamlit — construct the analysis from real or rigorously modelled data, not a template.
Argue Both Sides
State the strongest case for the conclusion, then the strongest case against it. If it can't survive its own rebuttal, it isn't a conclusion yet.
Ship the Verdict
A clear recommendation, reasoned on statistical and practical significance — not just correlation.
