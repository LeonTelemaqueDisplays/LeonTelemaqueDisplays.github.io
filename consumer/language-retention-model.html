Behavioural Insight
The Tourist is Duolingo's core acquisition demographic and its most difficult retention challenge simultaneously. High downloads, low lifetime value. The streak mechanic is the only thing keeping this user engaged — which means the streak breaking is the only thing needed to lose them permanently.
