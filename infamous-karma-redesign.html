INFAMOUS 2
KARMIC WORLD REACTIVITY

Infamous 2 has a karma system with a spine but no nervous system. Player actions feed a meter, the story responds, but the world in between — its factions, its civilians, its streets — stays deaf to who Cole has become. This redesign builds the nervous system.

⚡ Fortress Mission — Permanent Consequence
Taking the Ionic Storm path resets the Rebel faction meter to 0 instantly. The survivor becomes a permanent enemy. Switched players can rebuild to Stage 3 only through the Rebel Redemption Questline — freeing Rebel prisoners non-lethally. The survivor watches the final mission. He does not speak. Stage 3 unlocks. He does not forgive.

06 — Design Principles
01
The world reads pattern, not intent. Cole can't fake redemption or corruption with a single act. The world responds to consistent behaviour over time — not the moment, but the arc.
02
Same power, different character. Evil Cole isn't stronger — he's equally powerful but uncontained. Good Cole's mastery makes him more efficient. The destruction is a consequence, not a feature.
03
Consequences that don't dissolve. The fortress mission zeroes the Rebel meter permanently. Redemption is possible — but it has to be earned through the specific act that mirrors what was broken.
04
Faction meters trail personal karma. Each faction transitions at its own speed. The world is always slightly behind Cole — which makes the shift feel earned rather than instant.
05
Civilians communicate through presence, not broadcast. Factions have a voice. Civilians have a body. Their fear or inspiration is felt in the world — not announced.
06
The grind is the arc. Point values are tuned so players feel Cole's transformation alongside him — not farming karma, but living it. Turning points are earned through difficulty, not repetition.
