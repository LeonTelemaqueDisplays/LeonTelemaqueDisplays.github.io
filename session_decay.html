From New Player
to Churned — The GTA Online
Session Decay Model

GTA Online has one of the most well-documented player lifecycle arcs in gaming. This model maps session frequency decay across 5 behavioral stages — from first login to churned — identifying the exact inflection points where engagement collapses and when intervention is viable vs. too late.

Core Insight
Session decay doesn't happen gradually — it happens in discrete drops at lifecycle transition points. New players who don't reach the Grinder stage within 14–21 days churn at 68%+. Players who do reach it become extremely sticky. The GTA VI window doesn't threaten Grinders directly — it threatens Coasting players who lose their reason to stay before they re-engage.

Player Lifecycle Stages
Stage 01
New Player
Day 0 → Day 14
Tutorial completion, first heist access, intro missions. High novelty — open-world sandbox exploration drives sessions. Decision window: does the player find a motivation loop (money, PvP, social) within 2 weeks?
Sessions: 4–6/week · 1.2h avg
Stage 02
Discovering
Day 14 → Day 60
First property purchase, heist crew formation, business setup. Players begin understanding the income loop. First economic friction contact point — grind hours start feeling real. D30 cliff happens here.
Sessions: 3–5/week · 1.5h avg
Stage 03
Grinder
Month 2 → Month 12
Optimal income loop active (Cayo + businesses). Daily sessions often mandatory to collect passive income. Highest session frequency and length. Most resistant to churn — sunk cost in assets and time is at peak.
Sessions: Daily · 2.1h avg
Stage 04
Coasting
Month 12 → Month 36+
Major assets acquired. No clear new goal. Sessions become shorter and less frequent — logging in for events, weekly bonuses, or social reasons. GTA Online's largest segment by count. Most vulnerable to GTA VI disruption.
Sessions: 2–4/week · 1.0h avg
Stage 05
Churned
14-day+ gap → exit
Session gap exceeds 14 days. Login rate drops below weekly. 68% of players with a 7+ day gap don't return within 30 days. GTA VI as a catalyst compresses the Coasting → Churned transition from months to weeks.
Sessions: <1/month · churn probability 68–91%

Technical Artifact
SQL — Session Decay Scoring + Lifecycle Stage Classification Portfolio Artifact · Powers ChurnScore in Page 01
-- Session Decay Model: Lifecycle Stage + Decay Score for GTA Online -- Output feeds into ChurnScore.session_decay_90d in operation_last_ride.html WITH session_history AS ( SELECT player_id, COUNT(*) AS total_sessions, MIN(session_date) AS first_session, MAX(session_date) AS last_session, DATEDIFF(MAX(session_date), MIN(session_date)) AS tenure_days, DATEDIFF(CURRENT_DATE(), MAX(session_date)) AS days_since_last, /* Weekly session rate — decay signal */ COUNT(*) / GREATEST(1, DATEDIFF(MAX(session_date), MIN(session_date)) / 7.0) AS sessions_per_week, /* Recent vs early session rate ratio — measures decay slope */ SUM(CASE WHEN session_date >= DATEADD('day',-30,CURRENT_DATE()) THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / GREATEST(1.0, SUM(CASE WHEN session_date < DATEADD('day',-30,CURRENT_DATE()) AND session_date >= DATEADD('day',-60,CURRENT_DATE()) THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) ) AS recent_vs_prior_ratio FROM player_sessions GROUP BY player_id ), lifecycle_stage AS ( SELECT *, CASE WHEN days_since_last >= 14 THEN 'Churned' WHEN days_since_last >= 7 THEN 'At-Risk' WHEN tenure_days > 365 AND sessions_per_week < 3 THEN 'Coasting' WHEN tenure_days > 60 AND sessions_per_week >= 5 THEN 'Grinder' WHEN tenure_days > 14 AND sessions_per_week >= 2 THEN 'Discovering' ELSE 'New Player' END AS lifecycle_stage, /* Decay score: 0=healthy, 1=fully decayed */ LEAST(1.0, GREATEST(0, (1.0 - LEAST(1.0, recent_vs_prior_ratio)) * 0.5 + (LEAST(1.0, days_since_last / 14.0)) * 0.5 )) AS session_decay_score FROM session_history ) SELECT lifecycle_stage, COUNT(*) AS players, ROUND(AVG(session_decay_score), 3) AS avg_decay_score, ROUND(AVG(sessions_per_week), 1) AS avg_sessions_wk, ROUND(AVG(days_since_last), 0) AS avg_days_since_login, ROUND(AVG(tenure_days), 0) AS avg_tenure_days FROM lifecycle_stage GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY avg_decay_score DESC;

Economy Model (Page 02)
Session decay and economic friction compound. Coasting players have low friction tolerance — when a 14h grind appears at Stage 4, the probability of conversion drops to near zero. The D30 cliff is where economic friction first becomes a churn driver.
Key implication
GTA VI Compresses the Arc
In normal conditions, the Coasting → Churned transition takes months. With a GTA VI release in 169 days, Coasting players have a concrete exit date. The lifecycle arc compresses. Intervention must happen before the 7-day gap, not after.
